Parts that belong to others

These parts keep their own licences, which this page does not change and does not restrict:

  • The quest system and the dialogue system started from two Unreal Marketplace plugins, and the main menu, settings and save screens from an Unreal Marketplace asset; all were modified for the game. They are used under the Marketplace's licence terms.
  • Sprite animation uses the PaperZD plugin.
  • Pixel textures were generated with Stable Diffusion using community pixel-art LoRAs and a pixel-style SDXL base model, then cleaned up on the models' UVs in Photoshop.
  • The music and sound effects in the gameplay demo video are by other creators; most are CC0.
  • The project film's narration is a synthetic voice made with the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (Apache License 2.0).
  • The project film's music and sound effects were written for it in code. They play instrument samples from VSCO 2 Community Edition, the Versilian Community Sample Library and FreePats, all dedicated to the public domain (CC0).
  • The project film's pixel font, text boxes and frames come from the code of the Lemo-Opuscar library by Lemomo (LemoLab), with a few added glyphs, under the MIT License.
